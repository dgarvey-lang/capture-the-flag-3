Capture the Flag: Systems of Equations

Solve problems from the 3-1 practice worksheet to move your runner across the field. Reach the other team’s flag first to capture it!

🌐 Live class game

Everyone in class plays on their own computer. Pick Red or Blue, and every correct answer moves your team’s runner.

👥 Two teams, one screen

Project it or share one computer. Red and Blue teams take turns. The team at the keyboard answers each question.

🏃 Solo practice

Race a rival runner on your own. It moves forward every 40 seconds, so answer quickly and carefully.

How it works. Each question comes from the worksheet. Fill in your work, then press Check answer. A correct answer moves your runner one step. A wrong answer tags you and you wait a few seconds. Help gives a hint first, and another press shows the full solution, but you don’t earn a step on that question. Once you’ve seen every worksheet problem, you’ll get new similar problems with different numbers.
